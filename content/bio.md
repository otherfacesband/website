<b>Other Faces</b> is [Dan Langa](https://www.danlanga.com/), [Kristian de Leon](https://kristiandeleon.com), and [Adam Lutz](https://www.adamlutzmusic.com/), a multimedia electroacoustic trio fusing visual and auditory textures into radiant, expansive work. Bound by a shared curiosity to push tonality into textural and visual territory, the trio builds its music through sonic exploration and meticulous electronic crafting. Other Faces was a 2024–25 Sō Percussion Studio Residency Partner in Brooklyn and are releasing their self-titled debut LP on cmntx records in November 2026.
